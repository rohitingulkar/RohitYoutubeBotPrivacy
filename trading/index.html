Trading Channel Uploader

A private tool by Rohit Ingulkar that creates and publishes educational videos on his own YouTube channel, Trading Strategies by Rohit (@rohitingulkar).

About the channel

Trading strategies for every market: Indian stocks, Nifty and Bank Nifty, options, forex, crypto and commodities. Each video teaches one rules-based setup and when it fails. Educational content only, not financial or investment advice; no buy or sell calls.

What this app does

  • Uploads the channel's own videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions on a schedule.
  • Reads the channel's own analytics (views, watch time, retention) to improve future videos.
  • Replies to public comments on the channel's own videos.
  • Keeps the channel's own About description up to date.

It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to other users.
