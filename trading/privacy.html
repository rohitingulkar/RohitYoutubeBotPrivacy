Privacy Policy

Trading Channel Uploader · last updated 25 October 2026

The Trading Channel Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by Rohit Ingulkar to create and publish videos on his own YouTube channel, Trading Psychology by Rohit (@rohitingulkar). It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the owner's channel;
  • read the owner's own channel and video information and analytics (views, watch time, retention);
  • read public comments on the owner's own videos and post replies as the channel;
  • update the owner's own channel description and keywords.

The app does not access any other user's account or private data.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to run and improve the owner's channel. OAuth tokens, video files and channel statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Video scripts are written with the help of the Anthropic API; the only YouTube data sent to it is the text of public comments being replied to and public video titles used for research.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local data is kept only as long as needed to run the channel. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and stored tokens can be deleted from the app's credentials folder.

Financial content

Videos published by the app are educational only and are not financial or investment advice.
