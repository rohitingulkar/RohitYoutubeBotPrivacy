Chess Channel Uploader

A private tool by Rohit Ingulkar that creates and publishes chess lessons on his own YouTube channel, Chess Strategies (@ChessMistake).

About the channel

Chess strategies, openings and traps for club players, plus fresh ideas from top-level chess. Short lessons Monday to Friday and a full video every Sunday.

What this app does

  • Uploads the channel's own videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions on a schedule.
  • Reads the channel's own analytics (views, watch time, retention) to improve future videos.
  • Replies to public comments on the channel's own videos.
  • Keeps the channel's own About description up to date.

It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to other users.
